# Tamplan Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Tamplan is a day planner that lets you schedule your day with alarms, earn rewards for completed tasks, and split those rewards each month into expenses, savings and investment.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Tamplan has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

## Data stored on your phone

Your schedule, tasks, rewards and settings are saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export it.

- **Backups**: if you tap *Export backup*, Tamplan creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. You decide where that file goes, for example your Files app, email or a cloud drive. The file is then handled by whichever app or service you send it to.
- **Restoring**: if you tap *Restore from backup*, Tamplan reads only the file you pick.

## Notifications and alarms

Tamplan asks permission to show notifications so your alarms and the month-end reminder can ring. Alarms are scheduled locally by your phone's operating system. No notification content is sent to us or to anyone else.

## Deleting your data

Go to **Settings → Erase all data** to delete everything immediately, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing else to delete.

## Children

Tamplan does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
